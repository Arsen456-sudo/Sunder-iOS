## Design
Isolate chat profile destination into a separate View. The route closure explicitly
captures only Chat/model/theme and necessary state bindings, not the parent ChatView
or its DismissAction environment. Keep profile content/back/privacy unchanged.
Defer root visibility model publication out of UIKit view lifecycle reconciliation,
with a generation guard; retain immediate imperative native tab hiding. Skip equal
hiddenRoots writes to avoid redundant ObservableObject invalidations.
Resolve native toolbar items from public leading/trailing/centerItemGroups plus legacy
arrays, deduplicated. Identify source filter/compose and destination avatar by semantic
accessibility IDs/labels rather than group order. Assign only existing actual items.
Do not create/move center groups or change the visible title. Logs prove titleView
is not a native center bar item; exact filter-title morph still cannot be claimed.
